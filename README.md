# Data-Analysis-of-pizza-sale-report
Comprehensive Sales Analytics Project
Tools Used: SQL, Power BI, Excel
# Description:
Developed a fully functional sales analytics dashboard by integrating and analyzing transactional data using SQL, Power BI, and Excel. The project followed a complete data analysis lifecycle:

🔍 Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:
Cleaned and standardized raw sales data in Excel and SQL, handling missing values, date formats, and inconsistencies across product categories and sizes.

🧮 Data Transformation:
Performed aggregations and joins using SQL to prepare structured tables for dashboard reporting (e.g., revenue by category, daily sales trends).

📊 Interactive Dashboard Development:
Designed a comprehensive dashboard in Power BI including:

Total Revenue, Orders, and Pizzas Sold

Average Order Value (AOV)

Best-selling Pizzas & Categories

Sales by Time (Hour, Day, Category, Size)
# KPI & Insight
1. Total Revenue
Definition: Sum of all total_price.

✅ Useful to track overall sales performance.

2. Total Orders
Definition: Count of unique order_id.

✅ Reflects number of transactions.

3. Total Pizzas Sold
Definition: Sum of quantity.

✅ Indicates total units sold.

4. Average Order Value (AOV)
Formula: Total Revenue / Total Orders

✅ Measures how much each customer spends on average.

5. Top 5 Best-Selling Pizzas
Based on: Highest quantity sold per pizza_name

✅ Helps identify customer favorites.

6. Sales by Pizza Category
Breakdown of revenue or quantity by pizza_category (e.g., Classic, Veggie, Supreme)

✅ Useful for product mix and marketing focus.

7. Sales by Pizza Size
Shows performance by size: S, M, L, XL, etc.

✅ Guides pricing and inventory decisions.

8. Peak Sales Days
Analyze order_date to find the busiest days.

✅ Helps with staffing and stock planning.

9. Hourly Sales Trend
Use order_time to identify peak hours.

✅ Optimize operations and delivery scheduling.

10. Revenue by Ingredient (optional advanced)
Explores contribution of popular ingredients (needs more data wrangling).

✅ Could be used to drive promotions.

Delivered actionable insights on peak sales hours, high-performing products, and customer behavior, providing value for business decision-making.
